Before we begin

Your questions,
answered.

Can you help a med spa that hasn’t opened yet?+

Yes. Foundation starts with positioning, identity, a website and your booking journey. We then plan local visibility and launch activity around your opening date.

Do we have to replace our website or booking software?+

We review what works first. We connect your existing tools where they support the plan; any redesign, migration or new software is agreed with you before it becomes part of the scope.

What does AI-search visibility actually include?+

Clear treatment content, consistent business information, structured data and monitoring how your practice appears in search and AI answers. We don’t promise rankings, recommendations or access to any platform’s advertising inventory.

Will AI give patients medical advice?+

No. We scope AI around practice-approved administrative questions, enquiry routing, follow-up and booking. Clinical or treatment suitability questions go to your qualified team. Voice and messaging workflows use the permissions and consent appropriate to your setup.

How do you measure progress?+

We agree on the measures before starting. These may include relevant enquiries, response times, booked consultations, attendance and return visits, depending on what your systems can report.

What should we expect in the first 90 days?+

A scoped plan, the agreed foundations and campaigns, regular learning and a review of what to improve next. Launch timing and results depend on your starting point, approvals, market, offer, budget and capacity.

Who approves our treatment content and imagery?+

Your clinical team approves treatment information and claims. We agree image rights, patient permissions and the review process before publishing your campaigns. The clinic scenes on this page are generated illustrations, not patient testimonials or client work.
